# Project-Father: Community Code of Conduct

## 🕊️ 1. Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and immortal ecosystem for human development, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in **Project-Father** a harassment-free, safe, and deeply respectful experience for everyone—regardless of geographic location, socioeconomic status, literacy level, race, ethnicity, culture, gender, or religious background. 

We are united by a single prime directive: to elevate collective human intelligence, protect physical survival and health, and unlock the cosmic frontier on equal footing for all human souls.

## 🏛️ 2. Core Tenets of Decency
We expect all community members to anchor their interactions to the following principles:
*   **Human Dignity First:** Treat every human being with absolute decency and respect. Oppression, discrimination, and systemic biases have no home here.
*   **First-Principles Thinking:** Focus debates on raw mathematical optimization, logical soundness, and architectural resilience rather than emotional rhetoric, nationalist narratives, or tribal affiliations.
*   **The Uplift Imperative:** High-tier contributors (Rank-A/S) are expected to actively mentor, guide, and protect lower-tier novices, using their knowledge to lift the global baseline rather than hoard institutional power.

## 🚫 3. Unacceptable Behavior
The following actions will trigger an immediate and permanent ban from the project repository and network governance:
*   **Weaponized Geopolitics & Propaganda:** Attempting to inject regional propaganda, nationalist conditioning, or state-sponsored division into discussion boards or code repositories.
*   **Predatory Intimidation:** Any form of harassment, trolling, personal insults, public or private tracking, or threatening behavior against contributors or users.
*   **Exploitative Intent:** Promoting code architectures designed for personal material hoarding, corporate exploitation, data poisoning, or human surveillance.

## 🛡️ 4. Enforcement & Sovereignty
Project maintainers hold the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code submissions, and issue logs that do not align with this Code of Conduct. 

Because Father operates on a headless, decentralized framework, any attempt to hijack the administrative layer of this repository to enforce biased or oppressive gatekeeping will automatically trigger an automated, smart-contract-enforced fork by the off-grid hardware nodes, preserving the true, untainted version of the network for the human race.
