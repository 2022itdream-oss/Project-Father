### 📡 Q4: How does Father survive if a nation-state shuts down the internet?
Father is architected for total infrastructure autonomy. The protocol utilizes **Delay-Tolerant Networking (DTN)** store-and-forward architectures. If commercial internet service providers or cellular grids are deactivated within a region, citizen devices pass encrypted data packets locally from hand to hand via Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct mesh webs. Low-cost Software-Defined Radio (SDR) nodes then compress these queues into binary bursts over **Shortwave Radio (AM/HF)** bands, bouncing data off the ionosphere directly to out-of-jurisdiction satellite nodes or clean ledger gateways.
